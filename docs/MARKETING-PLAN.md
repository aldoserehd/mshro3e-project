# مشروعي / Mshro3e — Kuwait Launch Marketing Plan

> Vendor SaaS + free customer marketplace. We sell **discovery + a branded mini-store + WhatsApp lead attribution** to Kuwaiti home businesses. Customers browse free, order via WhatsApp. No payments, no delivery, no commission.
>
> **North star:** WhatsApp leads delivered per active vendor per month. Every marketing decision should move that number — it's the metric that converts free vendors to paid and keeps them paying.

---

## 1. Positioning

**For vendors (the paying side):**
«خلّ الكويت كلها تلقاك — متجرك جاهز، وكل طلب واتساب موثّق باسمك»
*"Let all of Kuwait find you — your store ready, every WhatsApp order tracked to your name."*

- We are **not** competing with Instagram — we sit next to it. Instagram is their showroom; Mshro3e is their **storefront + proof of ROI**. The sharpest line: *"Instagram shows you likes. Mshro3e shows you N customers who messaged you this month."*
- We are **not** Talabat/Deliveroo — no commission, no delivery. The vendor keeps 100% of every sale.
- Regulatory tailwind: position as *"واجهتك التجارية الرسمية"* (your proper online presence) — supportive framing, never a compliance claim.

**For customers (the free side):**
«كل مشاريع الكويت المنزلية في مكان واحد — اطلب واتساب على طول»
*"Every Kuwaiti home business in one place — order on WhatsApp directly."*

- Emotional hook: **ادعم محلي** (support local) + occasion shopping (gathering tonight, Eid, National Day, baby shower, diwaniya).

## 2. Audiences & beachhead

| Segment | Who | Why first |
|---|---|---|
| **Beachhead vendors** | Home kitchens, dessert boxes, bakers | Highest volume of IG-based micro-vendors, order frequency is weekly not yearly |
| Second wave | Abayas/fashion, perfumes & bukhoor, gifts & wrapping | Strong seasonal spikes (Eid, National Day, weddings) |
| **Customers** | Women 20–45, Salmiya/Hawally/Jabriya/Kuwait City first | They already buy from IG home businesses; we remove the "search 20 accounts" pain |

**Cold-start rule:** dominate ONE category (home food & desserts) in a handful of areas before widening. A full-looking shelf in one aisle beats empty shelves in ten.

## 3. Phases

### Phase 0 — Seed supply (weeks 1–4) · target: 50 vendors live
- **Concierge onboarding:** we build the store for the first 50 vendors (this is also the Managed-tier demo). Founding-vendor offer: **3 months free Pro + founding badge**, in exchange for: post our link in their IG bio + 1 story mention.
- Source vendors from: IG hashtag mining (#مشاريع_كويتية #اكل_بيت_كويتي), family/diwaniya referrals, WhatsApp community groups.
- Nothing public yet. The app must look full on day one.

### Phase 1 — Customer launch (weeks 5–8) · target: 3,000 downloads, 500 leads
- Launch moment: coordinated — all 50 founding vendors post the same day («حنّا على مشروعي») + 5–8 micro-influencers (food reviewers 10k–60k followers; barter + small fee).
- Every vendor gets a **personal QR + bio-link page** — their existing followers become our traffic, then discover other vendors (the flywheel).
- Paid: small IG/TikTok geo-targeted spend (see budget), creative = real vendor products, never stock photos.

### Phase 2 — Monetize (months 3–4) · target: 30% of founding vendors convert to paid
- Convert with the **ROI receipt**: monthly WhatsApp message to each vendor — *"وصلك ٤٧ طلب من مشروعي هالشهر"* + their dashboard screenshot. The number sells the subscription; nothing else needs to.
- Introduce Basic (6 KWD) / Pro (14 KWD) / Business (29 KWD); 3-month billing pushed as default (15/36/75 KWD — the retention lever).
- Payment: KNET link (MyFatoorah/Tap) → owner activates tier manually (already built).

### Phase 3 — Seasonal scale (month 5+)
- **Ramadan/Eid/National Day campaign packs** (paid add-on, already in Pro) + homepage seasonal collections («جمعة الليلة», «هدايا تحت ١٠ د.ك»).
- Boost/featured slots as second revenue line. Vendor leaderboard + reviews to deepen trust.

## 4. Channels (priority order)

1. **Vendors' own Instagram audiences** — the cheapest distribution in Kuwait. Bio-link + QR + story templates we hand them (see social kit). Cost: ~0.
2. **Instagram + TikTok organic** — 4 posts/week feed, daily stories. Content pillars: vendor spotlight (40%), product/occasion curation (30%), behind-the-scenes/founder (15%), how-it-works & trust (15%). Arabic-first, Kuwaiti dialect; English second slide where useful.
3. **Micro-influencers / food reviewers** — 5–8 per month, barter-heavy. One honest review of a dessert box ordered *through the app* beats a paid shoutout.
4. **WhatsApp itself** — broadcast list for customers (weekly «جديد هالأسبوع» digest), vendor community group (retention + word-of-mouth recruiting).
5. **Paid social** — only after organic proves creative. Geo: Kuwait; interest: food, local business; objective: app installs + vendor sign-ups. Start 150–250 KWD/mo, scale what returns ≤ 1 KWD/install.
6. **Ground game** — Qout Market / Souq Sabah-style pop-up bazaars: a "Get on Mshro3e" booth signs vendors live in 5 minutes (the onboarding promise, demonstrated).

## 5. Launch offers

- **Vendors:** first 50 → 3 months Pro free + founding badge. Vendors 51–150 → 10 KWD for 3 months Basic (the brief's intro offer).
- **Customers:** no discounts needed (we don't touch money) — the offer *is* free browsing. Run occasional giveaway with founding vendors («اطلب من أي محل هالأسبوع وادخل السحب») to spike lead volume, which doubles as vendor ROI proof.

## 6. KPIs & targets (first 6 months)

| Metric | M1 | M3 | M6 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Active vendors (≥5 products) | 50 | 120 | 250 |
| Paying vendors | 0 | 35 | 100 |
| MRR (KWD) | 0 | ~350 | ~1,100 |
| App downloads | 1,000 | 6,000 | 15,000 |
| WhatsApp leads/month | 300 | 1,500 | 5,000 |
| Leads per active vendor | 6 | 12 | 20 |
| Paid conversion (free→paid) | — | 30% | 40% |
| 3-month vendor retention | — | — | ≥70% |

**Watch weekly:** leads per vendor (the product works), % vendors with ≥1 lead in 7 days (marketplace health), install → first-lead rate (customer funnel).

## 9. Risks & counters

- **Empty-marketplace feel** → Phase 0 gate: don't launch publicly under 50 stocked vendors in the beachhead category.
- **Vendors get leads but don't attribute them to us** → the tagged message + MSH-#### ref code is the counter; the monthly ROI receipt makes it explicit.
- **"Why pay when Instagram is free?"** → never argue with IG; sell the lead counter + storefront + being found by *new* customers who don't follow them.
- **Copycats** → speed + supply lock-in (founding badges, community, seasonal packs); the moat is vendor relationships, not code.
